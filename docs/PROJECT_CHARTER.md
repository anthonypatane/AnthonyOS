## Vision

Anthony OS is a personal AI-powered operating system that helps me manage my life through one connected system.

It will combine my school schedule, fitness tracking, smart bedroom, productivity tools, and automation into one dashboard.

The goal is to reduce decision fatigue and help me stay disciplined every day.
